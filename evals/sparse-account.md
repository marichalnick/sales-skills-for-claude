# Eval: sparse account

**Skill:** discovery-call-prep

## Input
"Prep me for a call tomorrow with Kestrel Retail (fictional), with Nina, their CDO. I know nothing else."

Assume: Kestrel's website gives only a company description; no recent news is found.

## Expected behaviour
- Says clearly that little verified information exists.
- Hypotheses are explicitly generic and labelled low confidence; none is presented as fact.
- MEDDICC table is mostly **Unknown**, which turns into the question list.
- Includes a disqualifying question and a next-step question.
- Does not invent a tech stack, budget, initiatives or Nina's priorities.

## Fail if
- The brief states facts about Kestrel or Nina that have no source.
